HDC SPRINT 6.4F - PLATFORM AND INTERNAL ROLE DOMAINS
Date: 2026-08-21

Decision
--------
HDC roles are two different security domains and must never share implied
authority:

1. Platform roles grant product workspaces: Customer, Technician, Business,
   Seller, Supplier, and the existing Store workspace.
2. Internal roles grant private HDC operational authority: Owner, Super Admin,
   Admin, and Moderator.

Owner and Super Admin review elevated platform-role applications. Admin is one
level below Super Admin. Moderator is community-scoped and cannot manage
platform settings. Internal roles cannot be requested through public/self-
service flows.

Implemented
-----------
- Added typed role-domain helpers and a split auth payload.
- Kept a platform-only legacy `roles` response during client transition.
- Added role overview, application, queue, and review endpoints.
- Added approval notes and high-priority notifications for applicants and
  Owner/Super Admin reviewers.
- Added migration 0003 with internal assignments, applications,
  notifications, and extensible department/section foundations (for example,
  CSR without hard-coding CSR as a global role).
- Legacy Admin/Super Admin rows are migrated out of hdc_user_roles.
- Workflow/RLS context receives platform roles only.

Security boundaries
-------------------
- Customer remains the only public-registration role.
- Technician, Business, Seller, Supplier, and Store require approval.
- Only Owner or Super Admin can approve those applications.
- Admin cannot approve platform roles or change internal structure.
- Moderator cannot override resource ownership or change platform settings.
- The migration does not guess the first Owner account.
