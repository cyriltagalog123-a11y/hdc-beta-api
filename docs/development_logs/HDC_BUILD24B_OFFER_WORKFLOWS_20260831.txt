HDC BUILD 24B - OFFER WORKFLOWS AND OFFICIAL BRAND MARK
Date: 2026-08-31

Scope
- Migrates Technician opportunities and request details to the responsive HDC
  flow language introduced in Build 23 and continued in Build 24A.
- Adds a guided proposal-studio header, progress state, and compact-safe draft
  and submission controls.
- Migrates Customer offer overview, request-specific inbox, proposal details,
  and proposal comparison to the same responsive flow language.
- Removes horizontal proposal-comparison scrolling by using responsive stacked
  or multi-column cards.
- Adopts the approved blue HDC hexagon/network/H mark in the Flutter lockup,
  startup shell, favicon, and standard/maskable PWA icons.
- Optimizes the bundled logo and PWA PNGs to 8-bit delivery assets, reducing
  their combined footprint from about 3.9 MB to 0.84 MB without changing the
  approved artwork.
- Keeps comparison-selection controls stacked below compact breakpoints so the
  full action bar remains reachable on narrow phones.
- Aligns PWA theme colors with the HDC navy palette and treats the adjacent
  startup logo as decorative to avoid duplicate screen-reader announcements.

Preserved authority and safety boundaries
- No database schema or migration change.
- No authentication, authorization, transaction, payment, chat, document, or
  dispute behavior change.
- ProposalProvider and the existing proposal acceptance flow remain the source
  of truth for drafts, submission, shortlist, comparison, and acceptance.
- The one-offer-per-technician-per-request lock remains enforced.
- The logo is bundled locally; startup and app branding do not depend on a
  remote image host.

Required verification
- Flutter analyze, widget tests, and production web build.
- Node/TypeScript validation, migration checks, portability checks, release
  synchronization, secret-pattern scan, and PostgreSQL workflow tests.
