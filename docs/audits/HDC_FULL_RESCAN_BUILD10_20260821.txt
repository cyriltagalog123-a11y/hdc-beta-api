Outcome
-------
Private operational authority is no longer rendered or returned through public
Role Center or profile surfaces. Authorized staff use the same authenticated HDC
account to switch to a separate, server-authorized private operations dashboard.

Confirmed public boundary
-------------------------
- Public Role Center has no internal-role card, labels, permission explanation,
  private queue entry point, or staff authority names.
- Public Profile Center has no internal staff card, staff role labels, or
  department/section assignments.
- The public role-overview contract omits internal roles, private permission
  flags, and pending private review counts.
- The public profile-overview contract omits internal staff data.
- Dashboard headers continue to render platform-role labels only.
- An ordinary or Guest account receives no private-dashboard switch.

Confirmed private boundary
--------------------------
- The switch is derived from a backend-hydrated authenticated identity and does
  not change account, session, user ID, or login.
- Private dashboard data is loaded only after the authorized workspace opens,
  rather than being prefetched while the user remains on the public dashboard.
- GET /api/internal/dashboard rejects accounts without active internal access.
- Permission-filtered queries prevent community-only authority from receiving
  global member/workflow statistics or the platform-role approval queue.
- Active assignment/structure statistics exclude inactive departments and
  inactive assigned sections.
- Recent private activity omits audit metadata and user identifiers.
- The private response exposes permission capabilities rather than role names.
- Platform-role review remains protected again at its list/update endpoints.
